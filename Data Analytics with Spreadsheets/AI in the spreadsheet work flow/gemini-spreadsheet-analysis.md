# How Gemini Supports Spreadsheet Analysis

How AI (specifically Gemini) supports human analysts throughout the spreadsheet analysis process while keeping human judgment at the center.

## 1. The 5 Stages of the Spreadsheet Analysis Workflow

- **Understand the Problem** — Define the exact question you want to answer before opening a spreadsheet.
- **Prepare the Data** — Clean, structure, and format data (e.g., removing duplicates, fixing column labels).
- **Perform Calculations** — Use formulas, functions, and aggregations to calculate core metrics.
- **Explore Patterns** — Analyze the results for key trends, outliers, and relationships.
- **Communicate Insights** — Present findings clearly through summary tables, charts, or concise written reports.

## 2. How Gemini Assists at Each Stage

- **Problem Definition** — Brainstorming questions and identifying necessary data points.
- **Data Prep** — Recommending cleaning steps and spotting data quality issues.
- **Calculations** — Writing formulas, explaining complex functions, and debugging errors.
- **Pattern Exploration** — Helping describe observed trends or anomalies.
- **Communication** — Drafting clear summaries and simplifying technical jargon for non-technical audiences.

## 3. Why Human Judgment Remains Essential

- **Context Awareness** — AI does not know your organization's specific domain, goals, or nuances unless you provide them.
- **Accuracy Check** — AI can present incorrect or fabricated information with high confidence; every output requires human verification.
- **Ownership** — The analyst — not the AI tool — is accountable for the conclusions drawn and decisions made based on the data.
